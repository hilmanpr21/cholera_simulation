Meet Rafi.


Like many children in Bangladesh, Rafi goes to school every day.


Today, we will follow Rafi and help protect his neighbourhood from cholera.

Every day, Rafi goes to school from 8 am to 5 pm. At home and at school, he uses water from shared water sources for washing and consumption. When the water is clean (light blue), Rafi and his family stay healthy.

One day, Rafi becomes infected with cholera (Rafi turns red). As Rafi is infected, he contaminates the water at school and at home (it turns dark blue). When Rafi's family drinks contaminated water at home, his whole household becomes infected (the house turns red).

Rafi lives in a community with many neighbours. The children go to the same school and drink water from the same water source. Rafi and another student are infected with cholera, and contaminate the school water. The infected students then bring cholera back to their families. See how fast cholera spreads when more neighbours are infected.
